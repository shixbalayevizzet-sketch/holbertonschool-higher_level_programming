The 5-text_indentation module
=============================

Using the function
------------------

>>> text_indentation("Hello. How are you? I am fine: thanks.")
Hello.

How are you?

I am fine:

thanks.

>>> text_indentation("Holberton School.")
Holberton School.

>>> text_indentation("A.B?C:D")
A.

B?

C:

D

>>> text_indentation("  Hello.  World?  Yes: ok.")
Hello.

World?

Yes:

ok.
